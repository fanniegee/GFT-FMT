About the Clinic

Care with dignity at every stage

Guvaza Geriatric Clinic is being developed as an integrated health and geriatric care service within the wider Guvaza platform. Our vision is a welcoming environment where older people feel safe, respected and connected to the people who matter to them.

Dignity and independence

We aim to listen to each person's preferences, respect their privacy and support independence wherever possible. Care planning should recognise the whole person, including their daily routines, culture and relationships.

Families as partners

Our approach values communication with residents and their chosen family or representatives. Understanding a person's story helps shape support that feels familiar, compassionate and individual.

Coordinated support

The clinic's vision brings assisted living, frail care and health coordination together, within approved service arrangements and professional oversight.

A place to belong

Alongside personal support, our vision includes companionship, meaningful daily activities and a calm, accessible environment. We want families to understand the care pathway and feel able to ask questions at every stage.

The clinic is in development. Specific services, availability, staffing and admission arrangements must be confirmed with the clinic team before care is arranged.

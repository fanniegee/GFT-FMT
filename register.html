Beneficiary Registration

One registration record, one verified lineage chain, and one canonical beneficiary structure for the GFT portals.

Personal & Contact

Enter the applicant's legal/current details. Root surname is derived from the verified Guvaza lineage; it is not freely editable.

Document requirement will be determined from date of birth.

Family Lineage

The applicant must identify the biological parent and grandparent. The system verifies the parent against the authoritative family structure and derives the actual grandparent from that parent.

Parent ID and parent supporting document are optional. They are quick-proof evidence only and are never the sole condition for registration.

Family Structure

The system derives generation, branch and parent IDs. The applicant then selects WIFE 1–WIFE 8 manually. The correct choice is never disclosed after a failed attempt.

Two verification attempts are available. After the second incorrect attempt, family-structure verification is frozen and Founder/Admin review is required.

Identity & Documents

The required applicant document is selected automatically from age. Generation/document-era information may help select an appropriate historical template, but age determines the registration document requirement.

Accepted: PDF, JPG, JPEG or PNG. The browser checks file type/size/quality; OCR, authenticity and authoritative verification require the verification service.

Email Verification & Review

Email ownership is a separate security gate. It does not prove lineage. OTPs must be generated, hashed, rate-limited and verified by the backend.

ACCESS CONTROL

The right access for every role.

Clinic staff access is governed by server-side roles, not by browser settings.

  • GFT Founder: invites the GGC Super Admin and may invite subordinate clinic roles.
  • GGC Super Admin: can invite clinic administrators and department staff.
  • Clinic Administrator: invitation ability requires the explicit INVITE_STAFF permission.
  • Department staff: receive only their assigned access.

Invitations expire after 48 hours and can be used once. Never forward an invitation link to anyone other than its intended recipient.

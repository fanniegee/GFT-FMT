About the Clinic

Care with dignity at every stage

Guvaza Geriatric Clinic is being developed as an integrated health and geriatric care service within the wider Guvaza platform.

Assisted Living

A supportive environment for older people who require assistance while maintaining as much independence as possible.

Frail Care

Structured care for residents who require additional assistance because of frailty, disability or higher care needs.

Integrated Health

Health monitoring and care coordination within the scope of the clinic's approved services and professional oversight.

Our Services

A complete care pathway

The clinic's future online system will allow prospective clients and families to begin the care journey digitally.

01

Client Registration

Submit an online application for yourself or a family member and begin the clinic assessment process.

02

Assessment

Applications can proceed through the clinic's assessment and admission workflow before a resident is formally admitted.

03

Residential Care

Structured support for residents according to their assessed level of care.

04

Care Planning

Individual care planning will coordinate daily care, medical, nutrition and social care information.

05

Family Communication

Future family access will provide approved next-of-kin with appropriate resident updates and communication tools.

06

Clinic Services

The clinic will provide approved primary health services within its licensed scope.

Services & care pathways

Support shaped around the person

Our planned approach brings together daily support, residential care and coordination with appropriate health professionals. The team will confirm what can be offered following an individual assessment.

Assisted living

Support with daily routines while encouraging independence, choice and participation in community life.

Frail care

A structured support pathway for people with additional personal-care needs, subject to assessment and available facilities.

Health coordination

Communication and care coordination within the scope of approved services and the relevant professionals' responsibilities.

Application & assessment

Families can begin with an application, followed by discussion of needs, suitability and the next steps before admission is considered.

Personal care planning

A shared understanding of routines, preferences and support needs, reviewed with the person and their chosen representatives.

Family communication

Clear channels for questions, care discussions and planning, respecting the resident's privacy and consent.

These describe the clinic's intended care model, not a confirmation that every service is operating. Ask the team about current availability and assessment arrangements.
